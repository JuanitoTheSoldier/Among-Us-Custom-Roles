Introduction

Welcome to the Among Us Custom Roles Rulebook! This guide introduces new roles that enhance gameplay with unique abilities and mechanics. Whether you're a veteran or a newcomer, this rulebook will help you understand how each role works and how to win!

Base Rules of Among Us

Among Us is a game of teamwork and betrayal. Players are divided into two teams: Crewmates and Impostors.

  • Crewmates must complete tasks around the map or find and vote out all Impostors.
  • Impostors must eliminate Crewmates and sabotage their mission.
  • Meetings are called when a body is reported or an emergency meeting is triggered.
  • During meetings, players discuss and vote on who they believe is an Impostor.
  • The game ends when either all tasks are complete, all Impostors are eliminated, or the Impostors outnumber the Crewmates.

Crewmate Roles

Sheriff

Ability: Shoot another player. If they're an Impostor, they die. If not, you die. Cooldown: 25s

Engineer

Ability: Vent for up to 5 seconds. Cooldown: 20s

Swapper

Ability: Swap two players during a meeting. If one gets majority votes, the other dies instead.

Medic

Ability: Protect a player making them immortal until you die.
If you report within 15s of a murder, you know if the killer was light or dark colored.

Snitch

Ability: Finish tasks to reveal Impostors. When one task remains, Impostors learn who you are and your location.

Necromancer

Ability: Revive dead players in your radius. During revive (5s), all killers know your location. Max 3 revives per game. Revived players can't call meetings, and nor can you after reviving someone.

Neutral Roles

Executioner

Win by: Getting your target voted out. If they die another way, you become a Jester.

Jester

Win by: Getting voted out. Can vent but cannot travel between vents.

Killer Roles

Glitch

Abilities: Shapeshift (10s), Hack (10s disables player actions), Kill. All cooldowns: 25s

Arsonist

Ability: Douse players permanently. Ignite to kill all doused players in range. Douse Cooldown: 25s

Impostor Roles

Phantom

Ability: Turn invisible for 10s. Cooldown: 25s. Cannot kill while invisible.

Janitor

Ability: Clean dead bodies. Shares cooldown with kill action.

Morphling

Ability: Sample and morph into another player for 10s. Cooldown: 25s. Cannot vent.

Miner

Ability: Create custom vents (connect only to each other). Cooldown: 25s. Usable by impostors, jester and Engineer.

Undertaker

Ability: Drag bodies (speed reduced to 0.75x while dragging). Cooldown: 25s. Cannot vent.

Assassin

Ability: Guess someone's role during meetings. If correct, they die instantly. One use per game.

Underdog

Ability: Kill cooldown is 50s if partner alive, 12s if partner dead.

FAQ

Q: Can impostor roles work together?
A: Yes, all Impostors can coordinate, unless a role restricts an ability (like Morphling can't vent).

Q: Can Snitch see Neutrals?
A: No, Snitch reveals only Impostors.

Q: Can Necromancer revive Jester or Killer roles?
A: Yes, all roles can be revived.
